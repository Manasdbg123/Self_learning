Caching bridges the latency gap between fast in-memory execution and slower persistent disk access. Designing a distributed cache
requires precise eviction strategies and safeguards against cascading cache failures.
Caching Topologies Eviction Policies & Edge Mitigations

Write-Through: Writes update the cache and the primary DB triggering concurrent DB queries. Fix: Distributed mutex locking

risks data loss on crashes. TTLs.
